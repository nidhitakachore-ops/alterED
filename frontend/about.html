ABOUT ALTERED

Smart Education. Smarter Plan B.

alterED is a Smart Lecture Monitoring & Alternate Live Arrangement System designed to keep learning uninterrupted when a faculty member is unavailable.

Lecture Monitoring

Monitor scheduled lectures and faculty availability.

Smart Substitute

Find and assign suitable alternate faculty.

Live Arrangement

Provide alternate live lectures when required.

Instant Notification

Keep students and faculty updated instantly.
